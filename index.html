01 Overview

This Privacy Policy explains how Formkit (“the app”, “we”, “us”) handles information when you use it. Formkit is a locally installed Android application that resizes photos for exams, IDs, visas and documents, builds printable form sheets, and compresses PDFs.

The short version: the app does not collect, transmit, sell, or share your personal data. Everything you do stays on your device. The app does not request internet access.

02 Information we collect

We do not collect any personal information. Formkit has no user accounts, no sign-in, no server backend, and no analytics or advertising SDKs. We do not receive copies of your documents, photos, forms, or settings.

The following stay on your device and are never sent to us:

  • Images and documents you open, resize, convert, or compress.
  • Form presets, saved fields, and print sheet layouts you create.
  • App settings and preferences.

Because the app has no internet permission, it is technically unable to transmit this information off your device.

03 How your data is used

Your data is used solely to perform the actions you request — for example, resizing an image to a target DPI or file size, generating a print layout, or compressing a PDF. All processing happens locally on your device. Nothing is uploaded for processing.

04 Local storage

To make the app work between sessions, Formkit stores a small amount of data locally on your device:

DataWhere it is storedPurpose
Saved presets and form dataOn-device database (Room)Remember the forms and presets you create
Preferences and settingsOn-device preferences (DataStore)Remember your app settings
Exported filesWherever you choose via Android’s file pickerSave your results

This data is excluded from cloud backup, so it is not sent to Google’s or any other cloud service by the app.

05 Permissions

Formkit requests no sensitive Android permissions. When you choose a file, the app uses Android’s system file and photo pickers, which grant one-time access only to the file you select. The app does not browse or scan your storage in the background.

In particular, the app does not request internet, location, contacts, camera, microphone, SMS, or call permissions.

06 Sharing & third parties

We do not sell, rent, or share your data with anyone. There are no advertising networks, analytics providers, crash-reporting services, or third-party trackers embedded in the app.

If you use Android’s share or “open with” feature to send a file to another app, that action is initiated by you and is governed by the other app’s privacy policy.

07 Retention & deletion

Because data is stored only on your device, you control it completely:

  • Delete individual items inside the app where supported.
  • Clear the app’s storage in Android Settings → Apps → Formkit → Storage → Clear data.
  • Uninstall the app to remove all of its local data.

We cannot retain or delete data we never receive.

08 Children’s privacy

Formkit is a general-purpose utility and is not directed at children under 13. Because the app collects no personal information from anyone, it does not knowingly collect personal information from children.

09 Security

Your documents are processed and stored locally, so their security depends primarily on your device’s own protections (screen lock, encryption, and OS updates). We recommend keeping your device updated and using a screen lock. Because no data is transmitted to us, there is no server-side copy to be breached.

10 Your choices

You have full control over your data at all times: you can choose which files to open, where to export results, and when to delete app data. Since we hold no personal data about you, there is no request needed to access, correct, or export data from us.

11 Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy if the app’s features or legal requirements change. When we do, we will revise the effective date at the top of this page. Continued use of the app after an update means you accept the revised policy.
